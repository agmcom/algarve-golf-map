The Courses

Get ready to play your best game. Whether you choose the Ocean course or the Royal, you’re guaranteed an exceptional golfing experience. The stunning natural setting, ocean views and uniquely challenging holes are the epitome of golfing pleasure.

Ocean Golf Course

The Ocean course is composed of two of the original 9-hole golf courses designed by Sir Henry Cotton. It’s emblematic holes are two challenging and spectacular Par 4s, the 11th and the 14th – which treats golfers to some of the most spectacular views in the Algarve – and the Par 3 15th, which runs alongside the beach.

"This course is crammed with interesting and pretty holes and proved a worthy venue for the occasion. (…) The Ocean name begins to make perfect sense as you come over the top on 11 to be greeted by a stirring backdrop of gentle waves. You get closer still to the beach as you descend the 14th then play another signature par 3 along the shore, before making your way home on a closing par 5 where you play steeply up to a green blessed with a permanent grandstand."

JEREMY ELLWOOD (Contributing Editor - Golf Monthly) — OCTOBER 2023

Royal Golf Course

Recognised as one of Portugal’s most prestigious golf courses, its features include the almost island green of the 9th and the famous 16th, with cliff-top carryover – one of the most photographed holes in Europe.

Golf Etiquette

Vale do Lobo Golf Club welcomes all golfers: members, guests and visitors. To make sure every player has a great time with us, we ask that anyone enjoying our courses should read and follow our Club Rules.

Golf Tournaments

Whether you’re a complete beginner or a seasoned pro, we have a range of golf events and experiences that will ensure you play your best game.

Foursomes Week

A popular pairs competition in one of the finest golf communities in the Algarve. Since 1983, the Vale do Lobo Golf Club hosts a week of fun for foursomes on the resort’s courses that attracts a flurry of keen entrants, all sharing a hunger for quality golfing moments in spectacular surroundings. All participants are invited to take advantage of our special tournament package. It includes accommodation in one of Vale do Lobo’s beautiful properties, green fees unlimited golf over the competition week, and entry to the spectacular prize-giving dinner.

Foursomes Week 2027 Package

Amateur Week

Held over the last week of January each year, the Amateur Week provides a true golfing extravaganza. It’s all about competitive fun, making new friends and enjoying outstanding play in the resort’s beautiful natural surroundings or the welcoming community of the clubhouse. Vale do Lobo offers a special package for this tournament, including accommodation, green fees and unlimited golf over the week as well as entry to the closing ceremony and dinner.

Amateur Week 2027 Package
