     IMMOVABLE OBSTRUCTIONS (RULE 16)
Immovable obstructions include Sprinklers; Sprinkler Heads; Control Boxes; Drainage Grills; Manhole Covers; Wells (Cisternas); Permanent Buildings; Footpaths; Tyre Marks; Buggy Paths; Rocks; Wooden Posts (lining the side of buggy paths & service roads); The concrete ditch on the right-hand side of 8th Hole; Fences (except those marking out of bounds); Bridges; Netting including the fence as you approach the clubhouse /18th green. All of these are entitled to free relief.
FLOWER BEDS
If a ball comes to rest in the flower bed to the right of the 16th Hole or to the right of the 18th Green, then relief must be taken. The ball must be dropped within 1 club length of where the Drop Zone sign is placed, regardless of where the ball entered the flower bed.
TREE ROOTS
Whenever a ball comes to rest on a closely mowed area of fairway and is so close to a tree root that it interferes with your intended swing then, with all your playing companions’ agreement, you may drop the ball within a club length no nearer the hole. Your stance does not qualify for a free drop. The same rule applies when not on the fairway provided that the ball does not lie within a club length of a tree and the ball must then be dropped within a club length and in line with your intended line of play but no nearer the hole.
