Ombria golf course
Rules & Regulations
—
a. Access to the golf course is restricted to those who have a green fee ticket, that is issued by the reception.
b. Tee times are set, by default, with 10 minutes interval and only from Tee nº1, with a maximum of 4 players per tee time. Players may
be asked to join with other players to complete a group of 4 players.
c. Each player is required to use its own golf clubs/golf bag. Sharing of golf clubs / bags is not allowed.
d. The order of play is from tee #1 and holes must be played in the correct sequence. Changing the sequence or moving to other
holes is not allowed.
e. Pace of play is determined as 4h30 for 18 holes i.e. 2h15 for each nine holes.
f. Adequate golf attire is requested when at the golf course. Jeans are not allowed, shirts must have collar and sleeves (except for
ladies). Swimming trunks and short bermudas are not allowed. Appropriate golf shoes are requested, with soft spikes only. Tennis
shoes, open feet sandals or others are not allowed, unless it’s authorized by the management.
g. Green fees have VAT included and are always with buggy included (on a shared basis and subject to availability). Extra buggy may
be charged if client does not want to share the buggy (subject to availability).
h. The use of buggies is only allowed for those with 18 years old or more. The client will be responsible for any damage or loss of the
rental equipment (buggies, trolleys, clubs, etc). Clients can be asked to sign a term of responsibility upon check in.
i. Maximum handicap permitted: 28 EGA for men and 36 EGA for ladies.
j. No alcoholic beverages may be consumed on the Golf premises, including the grounds, except those that are provided by the
Ombria Golf Course, which means that personal coolers and beverages purchased elsewhere are prohibited. Ombria Golf Course staff
reserves the right to confiscate any personal coolers/beverages not purchased at the Golf Club. These items will be returned to the
guest after they conclude their round. The management and staff have the absolute right to refuse service to any player, member or
guest based on the individual’s apparent state of intoxication.
k. All players must respect the indications of the Reception team, Starter, Ranger or any of the Golf team and follow the golf course
rules and etiquette. Any one not complying with the indications will be asked to leave without any refund.
l. Reservations can be made by email, telephone or directly on Ombria’s website www.ombria.com. Information such as name, nationality,
handicap and email will be required. The reservation will only be valid once a confirmation email is sent by the reservations team.
m. Prepayment will be needed upon reservation or until the release date determined below.
n. Cancelations or alterations without charge:
i.5 days in advance for groups up to 8 players
ii.15 days in advance for groups from 9 to 16 players
iii. 30 days in advance for groups of more than 16 players
o. Bad weather and course closure. The golf management team may decide to close or suspend playing on the golf course whenever
there are situations presenting danger to the players and clients:
i.Thunderstorms: the golf management team will determine whether the course is safe to play and will sound a buzzer to
indicate that the play is SUSPENDED. The players must get back to the clubhouse and wait until further notice.
ii.The suspension will be assessed each 15 minutes and in the case the play is resumed, players can go back to where they
interrupted their round.
iii. No refund will be issued if there is still time to finish the round on that day.
iv. Heavy rain warning and suspension will be issued if at least 4 greens are completely flooded or if the management
considers that the golf course is unplayable.
p. Golf management can, at any moment, limit the buggies’ access to the golf course if needed.
q. Pets not allowed on the golf course.
