Cancellation and Payment Policy

Full pre-payment is required to guarantee and secure your booking.

Cancellations and Modifications

Cancellations or modifications without penalty may be made under the following conditions:

Up to 4 players: Cancellations and/or reductions may be made up to 15 days prior to the date of play at no charge.
5–23 players: Cancellations and/or reductions may be made up to 30 days prior to the date of play at no charge.
24 players or more: Cancellations and/or reductions will be subject to review and approval by the Events Coordinators and Golf Director.

No-shows, early departures, and any cancellations or modifications requested after the applicable deadline will be charged in full.

Important Information

No refunds will be issued for delayed or cancelled flights, personal injuries, or other circumstances beyond the control of the golf course.

Travel insurance is strongly recommended.
