Booking & Cancelation policy When making an online reservation, the client agrees to abide by the policies in use at the Boavista Golf Full payment of the green fees and any other item will be charged directly on the credit card. Online Green Fees and Buggies, once paid, cannot be changed on the date of play, at the reception. No promotional cards and/or discount vouchers can be accepted for online bookings Cancellations can only be accepted within the following deadlines: • Up to 4 players – 48 hours prior to date of play • Between 5 and 15 players - 72 hours prior to date of play • More than 15 players – 15 days prior to date of play A transaction fee of 2.00€ will be charged for any cancellations. In case of a refund the full amount (less the transaction fee) will be refunded within 5 working days to the same credit card. Golf Course Rules and Etiquette The golf course rules and etiquette are as follows: • Tee times will have 10 minutes interval. • Boavista Golf reserves the right to group four players per tee time. • Maximum EGA Handicaps permitted: Ladies 34; Men 26. • All players must wear appropriate golf shoes on the course with soft spikes. Metal spikes are not allowed on the golf course. • Dress code must be observed for the golf course and clubhouse as stipulated on the Course Regulations, no beachwear, no denims, no training shoes, no t-shirts, socks must be worn. • All players are responsible for any damage caused to any hired equipment during their round. • Each player must have his/her own golf bag with their own set of clubs. • A buggy may carry a maximum of two (2) players and two (2) golf bags and no person under the age of 18 may drive a buggy hired at Boavista Golf Course. Bad weather policy These are the conditions for the various situations that arise due to bad weather: THE COURSE MAY BE CLOSED FOR THE FOLLOWING REASONS: • Flooded greens; • Thunder, lightning or inclement weather *; • If the conditions are bad enough in any way as to be considered dangerous to the players or damaging to the golf course. * In inclement weather, a final decision will be made by the Golf Director or Course Superintendent. All players due to play golf on a day when the course is closed should firstly be offered alternative day of play. Any guests who are unable to accept an alternative day will be issued a rain check voucher. THE COURSE IS CLOSED AFTER THE GUEST HAS STARTED HIS/HER ROUND • If the course is closed after the guest has played less than 5 holes, a rain check voucher for 18 holes will be given. • If the client has played 5 or more holes a rain check voucher for 9 holes will be given.
