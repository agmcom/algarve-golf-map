 RULES
Dresscode
1 – The use of golf shoes at Pine Cliffs Golf Course is mandatory. Tennis, sandals or any kind of shoes not suitable for Golf are not allowed.
2 – Players should wear a shirt with a collar as well as appropriate trousers/shorts and golf shoes. Denim shorts or trousers, beachwear, tops, t-shirts and football style shirts are not permitted.
3 – Each player must have their own golf bag and clubs. Sharing golf bags is not permitted. 4 - Rental clubs and rental golf shoes are available on the golf shop.
Etiquette
1- Players must play without undue delay, the time allowed to play 9 holes is 2 hours.
2- To avoid undue delay, players whose score reaches double the par on any hole must pick up
their ball and play the next hole.
3- Two and three ball games have priority over four ballgames. A single player has no priority.
4- Players must replace their divots.
5- Players must clean the marks in bunkers and repair the pitch marks on the greens.
6- Trolleys are collected from the first tee and must be returned to the same place. It is not
permitted to leave trolleys at any other place on the resort.
7- Under 18 years of age only can play with the supervision of an adult.
8- Children under 3 years of age are not allowed to enter the golf course.
Local Rules
1 – Players without handicap must play from the RED markers.
2 - OUT OF BOUNDS is defined by the white stakes (rule 27 applies).
3 - GROUND UNDER REPAIR (rule 25 applies). All pathways within the bounds of the
course.
4 – IMMOVABLE OBSTRUCTION (rule 24.2 applies). The wire safety fence on the tee no6.
All courses furniture including the markers, distance and direction markers, sprinkler heads and irrigation control boxes. An area defined by the outer branches of any tree less then two meters in height.

Rules of Golf
1- OUT OF BOUNDS – Rule of Golf #27. The ball must be replayed from its original position. Penalty 2 shots (The original shot + one penalty).
GROUND UNDER REPAIR –Rule of golf #25. The ball must be dropped at the nearest point of relief, within one club length of the point of relief, not nearer the hole.
PENALTY NONE IMMOVABLE OBSTRUCTION. Rule of golf #24.2 The ball must be dropped within one club length of the point of relief (being the point that provides enough relief to take a stance and make a swing free of obstruction, plus one club length), not nearer the hole or at a point any distance behind the obstruction keeping the obstruction directly in line with the hole, or from the point from which the shot was originally played. PENALTY NONE.
