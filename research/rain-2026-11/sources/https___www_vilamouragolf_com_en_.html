OLD COURSE VILAMOURA
BEST GOLF COURSE
IN EUROPE

The Old Course Vilamoura, an essential part of vilamoura golf has been awarded as Europe’s Best Golf Course 2026 at the World Golf Awards.

This prestigious recognition not only celebrates the course’s heritage, its continuous improvements, and the dedication of the teams who maintain championship conditions, but also highlights Vilamoura Golf’s role as a premier golf destination in Europe, where world-class courses and unforgettable experiences come together.

VILAMOURA GOLF
YOUR GOLF DESTINATION
IN THE ALGARVE

Immerse yourself in the premier Portugal golf destinations, where five unique Golf courses await, each offering a challenge tailored to every golfer. Vilamoura Golf is the ideal escape, combining pristine fairways, rich history, and modern facilities, all within reach of Faro Airport. Whether you’re a seasoned player or a newcomer, Vilamoura promises an unforgettable golfing journey amidst the beautiful Portuguese coast.

Discover THE GOLF
IN VILAMOURA, PORTUGAL

Vilamoura is home to a collection of exceptional Golf courses, each offering a unique challenge and experience. From the charming fairways of Pinhal to the scenic landscapes of Laguna and Millennium, you’ll find endless opportunities to perfect your game. With a variety of courses to explore, Vilamoura truly stands out as a premier destination for golfers of all levels.
