Golf Booking Policies

  • Cancellation and Payment Policy

    Full payment is required to secure & guarantee your booking.

  • Cancellation & Modification | Terms & Conditions

    Groups of up to 4 players – Cancellations or reductions to numbers may be made up to 15 days before the date of play at no cost;

    5-23 players – Cancellations or reductions to numbers may be made up to 30 days before the date of play at no cost;

    24 or more players – Cancellations or reductions to numbers may only be made at the discretion of the Events Team or the Director of Golf;

    Any cancelations or modifications made after these time periods will incur full charges.

  • Important Information

    No refunds are provided because of delayed or cancelled flights;

    No refunds are provided because of personal injury;

    No refunds are provided in the event of inclement weather;

    Travel insurance is strongly recommended;

    Monte Rei Golf & Country Club accepts no responsibility for any loss or damage to golf equipment that is kept in our overnight golf storage facility.
