 THANK YOU FOR CHOOSING SALGADOS GOLF CLUB ! ** Did you know we have installed two (2) Foresight Sports professional golf simulators in our clubhouse? Come check them out along with our club room showing all golf and sporting events on our multiple big screen TV's. ** And don't miss our popular promotions (like our Hot Deals and Sundown specials which include a buggy!) so please check with us regularly for your next game. Specials like our Twilight and Sundown allow players to get in as many holes as possible until the course closes. When visiting us, please remember our policies: We want everyone to play and enjoy golf at Salgados, no matter the skill level, so keeping up with the group in front of you will be expected during your round. Clothing should include what is generally acceptable at upscale golf venues; changing clothes prior to playing will be needed if you are wearing T-shirts, lounge, gym or beach wear, offensive items, clothing with holes or articles that are in disrepair, etc. Closed shoes are also needed, as recycled water is used for irrigation. Our staff’s discretion and decisions are final and to be respected. Upcoming golf course maintenance: December 21st and 22nd (greens aerification - course closed)
